									<h3><span style="font-size:20px;"><span style="color:#003a70;">Try Them Out: 45-Day Certificates Are (Optionally!) Here</span></span><br>
									<span style="font-size:20px;"><span style="color:#003a70;">&nbsp;</span></span></h3>
									<span style="font-size:16px;">In preparation for the industry-wide shortening of certificate lifetimes, we changed the tlsserver profile to issue 45 day certificates on <a href="https://outreach.abetterinternet.org/e/1011011/on-may-13-20th-and-27th-247049/4fh7q/%%email_id%%/h/%%internal.tracker_url_hash%%">May 13</a>. This is part of our <a href="https://outreach.abetterinternet.org/e/1011011/2025-12-02-from-90-to-45/4fh7t/%%email_id%%/h/%%internal.tracker_url_hash%%">plan</a> to move all subscribers to 45 day certificates in 2028 but is completely optional now. If you’d like to get on with it and move straight to 45 days, or if you want to do some testing and experimentation (which we love and think of as a great use of our certificates!), try out the tlsserver profile!&nbsp;<br>
									<br>
									(And as a friendly reminder, shorter certificate lifetimes <a href="https://outreach.abetterinternet.org/e/1011011/02-24-rate-limits-45-day-certs/4fh7x/%%email_id%%/h/%%internal.tracker_url_hash%%">won’t affect your rate limits</a>!)&nbsp;<br>

									<h3><span style="font-size:20px;"><span style="color:#003a70;">Rustls Continues to Outperform the Competition</span></span></h3>
									<span style="font-size:16px;">The Rustls team published its Q1 2026 performance update, and the results speak for themselves: Rustls placed first in 14 of 16 benchmarks compared to OpenSSL and BoringSSL. The team is also previewing an exciting upcoming feature called "split mode," which could roughly double throughput for a single connection by enabling simultaneous sending and receiving on separate threads. Look for more in the Rustls 0.24 release later this year.<br>
