Qualified Charitable Distribution

If you are 70½ or older, you can give directly from your US-based IRA to ISRG without paying income tax on the distribution. This option is often called an IRA charitable rollover or a qualified charitable distribution (QCD). You can give up to an annual maximum set by the IRS, which adjusts each year, so confirm the current amount with your advisor.

Please seek the advice of your financial or legal advisor.

To make your gift, ask your IRA administrator to send the distribution directly to ISRG. Give them our legal name and federal tax ID so they can identify us. Please also let us know about your gift. Many IRA administrators send distributions without identifying the donor, so a quick note from you lets us thank you and honor your wishes for the gift.
